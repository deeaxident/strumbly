# Acoustic guitar recordings

Acoustic guitar samples from **tonejs-instruments**, by Nicholas Brosowsky,
distributed by Makefully Studios. Recording source: University of Iowa
Electronic Music Studios ("guitar ac - Iowa").

- Library: https://github.com/Makefully-Studios/tonejs-instruments/tree/main/samples/guitar-acoustic
- Original sources: https://github.com/nbrosowsky/tonejs-instruments/blob/master/sample-source-info.txt
- Sample license: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 (CC BY 3.0)
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

The library edited samples for consistency. Strumbly uses the E2, A2, D3,
G3, B3 and E4 MP3 recordings unmodified, with real-time pitch shifting
through Tone.Sampler for alternate tunings. No Salamander piano samples are used.
